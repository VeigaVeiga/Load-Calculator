Railway Cargo Securing and Lashing Plan Visualizer

Cargo securing is a critical part of safe rail freight preparation. Packages must be arranged and restrained so they do not slide, tip or shift during transport. Rail LoadForge provides a 3D workspace for visualizing a proposed cargo securing arrangement inside a container.

Plan and visualize the loading arrangement

The application includes visual placement of lashing belts, triangular timber, dunnage airbags and a container door net. These elements help communicate a proposed securing concept alongside the cargo layout and make it easier to discuss blocking, bracing and lashing requirements.

Understand weight and securing considerations

A securing concept should be considered together with cargo mass, dimensions, centre of gravity, contact surfaces, gaps and available securing points. Railway loading requirements depend on the cargo, equipment, route and applicable rules; consult UIC Loading Guidelines and carrier instructions.

Important operational limitations

Rail LoadForge is a visualization and planning aid. It does not calculate certified lashing forces or approve a securing design. Final securing must be assessed by qualified personnel against applicable rules and carrier requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Rail LoadForge for railway freight planning?

Yes. It is a browser-based tool for preparing and reviewing proposed railway container loading arrangements.

Does the tool replace professional approval?

No. It supports planning and visualization only. Confirm actual loading and securing with qualified personnel and the responsible carrier.
