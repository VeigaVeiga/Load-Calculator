Container Cargo Centre of Gravity Calculator

The centre of gravity (CoG) describes the balance point of a loaded cargo arrangement. In container and rail freight planning, cargo weight alone is not enough: the position of each package also affects the overall centre of gravity. Rail LoadForge calculates the combined cargo centre of gravity from weights and positions entered in the loading plan.

Plan and visualize the loading arrangement

For a group of cargo items, the combined centre of gravity is the weight-weighted average of their individual positions. On each axis, add each item's mass multiplied by its centre coordinate, then divide by total mass. The same principle applies independently along container length, width and height.

Understand weight and securing considerations

Two loading plans can have the same total cargo weight but different centre-of-gravity positions. Moving heavy packages toward one end or one side can shift the balance point. Reviewing the CoG helps planners understand the effect of cargo placement and compare alternative layouts.

Important operational limitations

Centre of gravity is one useful indicator, not a complete safety assessment. Also consider local floor loading, concentrated loads, container and wagon limits, axle or wheel loads where applicable, and route-specific requirements.

Rail LoadForge is a planning and visualization tool. Always verify final loading, securing and weight-distribution arrangements against applicable railway rules, equipment limits and carrier requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the combined cargo centre of gravity calculated?

For each axis, multiply each package's mass by the coordinate of its centre, add those products and divide by total cargo mass. The calculation is performed independently for length, width and height.

Does a centred cargo centre of gravity guarantee safe loading?

No. Centre of gravity is only one part of a loading assessment. Floor loading, concentrated loads, container and wagon limits, securing and route-specific requirements must also be checked.
